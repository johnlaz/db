How do I add my athlete's stats?
Go to the Stats tab, select your sport, and tap any stat to toggle visibility. Tap "Add custom stat" to add anything not on the list.
What is the Recruiter View?
The eye icon in the top bar opens a clean, polished profile page with a shareable link. Only items you've marked visible appear — parents control everything recruiters see.
What is a Persona?
Your persona is the narrative angle coaches will remember. Use the AI Wizard to build a custom one, or pick a quick-start style. It shapes the tone of every bio and caption the app generates.
Do I need a Groq API key?
Only for AI features — stat parsing from photos, persona generation, and coaching tips. The key is free at console.groq.com and is stored only on your device.
Is my data safe if I switch phones?
Use "Save backup" above to download your profile file, then "Restore from backup" on your new device. Everything transfers in seconds.
