How do I add my athlete's stats?
Open the Stats tab, pick the sport, and type each value. Stats left blank never appear on cards or pages. Tap Showing to hide one.
What does the profile preview show?
The eye icon opens the one-page sheet exactly as it prints and exports. Only items you have marked visible appear.
What is a persona?
An optional short label and paragraph about how your athlete plays. It stays off until you turn it on in the Persona tab, and you can create as many as you like.
Do I need a Groq API key?
Only for AI features: reading CSV stats and clear scoresheet photos, tagging photos, drafting a bio and generating personas. The key is free at console.groq.com and is stored only on this device.
Is my data safe if I switch phones?
Use "Save backup" above to download your profile file, then "Restore from backup" on your new device. Everything transfers in seconds.
