From the field to a coach's inbox.

Built for the family doing this on a phone, between games. No design skills needed.

Add what you have

Drop in a few photos, type the stats or import a CSV, and paste a link to a highlight video. Everything stays on your device and works offline.

Check it and polish

An optional AI key can read a CSV or a clear scoresheet photo and draft a bio. It only fills blanks, never invents facts, and you review every line.

Send it

Share the card as an image, print the one-page PDF, or export a profile website and host it wherever you like.

What a coach actually receives.

Drop in a scoresheet. Get stats to review.

With a Groq key, DraftBound reads CSV exports and clear scoresheet photos, fills only the blank stats and marks each one so you can check it before it goes anywhere.

A readiness check before you send

The app tells you what a coach would look for and what is missing: a cover photo, a highlight link, contact details, a GPA.

Your data stays with you

Profiles are stored on your device. Save a backup file to move to a new phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on AI or host an exported page.

A persona, only if you want one.

A persona is a short label and a few sentences on how your athlete plays. It is off by default. Turn it on, then write your own or generate as many options as you like.

You decide what goes online.

DraftBound exports files. Where they live, how long they stay up and who can see them is up to you. Exported pages ask search engines not to index them by default.
